About MindMapper

MindMapper is a powerful tool designed to help you organize and visualize your thoughts. Simply input any text content, and our advanced algorithms will generate a concise summary and an interactive mind map to help you understand the key concepts and their relationships.

Whether you're a student, professional, or just someone who loves to organize thoughts, MindMapper can help you transform complex information into clear, visual structures.
